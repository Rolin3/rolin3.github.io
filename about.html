About Tile Stack

A small, focused puzzle game built for players who love quick, satisfying challenges.

Our Mission

Tile Stack was created to offer a clean, fast, and genuinely fun match-3 puzzle experience without the usual friction of app stores, forced accounts, or aggressive monetization. We believe the best games are the ones you can pick up instantly and keep coming back to.

The design philosophy is simple:

  • Instant play: Open the site, tap play, and start matching.
  • Skill over luck: Every level can be cleared with the right strategy.
  • Respect your time: No unnecessary pop-ups, no artificial energy systems, no required sign-ups.

About the Creator

Tile Stack is developed by Rolin3, an indie creator who enjoys building small, polished web experiences. The game started as a weekend project inspired by the satisfaction of tile-matching mechanics and grew into a complete browser game with daily challenges, leaderboards, and a growing set of features.

Feedback from players drives every update. If you have ideas, bug reports, or just want to say hello, please reach out.
